# Murad Magharbeh — Design Brief

Design read: A cinematic architectural portfolio for clients, developers and collaborators seeking a Jordanian architect with international project experience.
Concept spine: The site is an architectural film reel: one controlled camera journey opens into a precise editorial archive of built and designed work.
Delivery tier: cinema.
Animation mode: animated-website.
Journey shape: single-shot, because one continuous architectural camera move creates a premium introduction without visual seams.
Journey: 1) Identity — MURAD MAGHARBEH / Architect, Designer, Project Leader. 2) Practice — architecture shaped by context, material and detail. 3) Reach — selected work across Jordan and international markets. 4) Portfolio — transition from cinematic world into project archive. 5) Contact — a quiet invitation to collaborate.
World grammar: Monumental contemporary architecture, pale limestone and warm concrete, soft daylight shifting toward blue hour, restrained landscape, crisp shadows, editorial luxury, no text in film, center-safe subject, negative space for typography.
Mobile framing: hero mass and primary architectural axis remain within the central 60% of frame; lighter vertical-safe encode.
Delivery budget: desktop film <=32 MiB, mobile film <=16 MiB.
Locked palette: #F2EFE8 limestone, #171816 ink, #5F625D muted sage-gray, #A5B0A0 mineral accent. Architectural and quiet, avoiding fashion-led warm metallic accents.
Locked type: Helvetica/Arial-style neo-grotesk for precision, with restrained uppercase display tracking. No decorative serif; architecture and imagery carry the character.
Section plan: cinematic hero journey; manifesto band; asymmetric selected-work grid; international practice statement; profile/about typographic split; contact/footer.
Asset plan: one continuous architectural film + exact-frame posters; supplied project imagery; typographic MM profile field; minimal MM monogram/favicon; OG cover derived from architectural imagery.
CTA inventory: View Selected Work uses an underlined directional text treatment; About Murad uses a framed text link; Contact uses a full-width architectural rule interaction.
SEO entity: Murad Magharbeh, Architect, Amman Jordan, architectural design, interior design, engineering, international projects.
Publishing: independent site only; no community-feed listing.
